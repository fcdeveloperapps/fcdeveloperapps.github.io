Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

This policy explains how the Kit & Kick app handles your personal data.

Who we are

The data controller is Federico Coppola, developer of the Kit & Kick app.

For any privacy request, write to: info.fc.developer@gmail.com.

What data we process

Kit & Kick is a football quiz game. You can play as a guest or create an account.

  • Account data: if you create an account, your email address, a password (managed by Firebase Authentication and not accessible to us) and a nickname of your choice. If you play as a guest, an anonymous identifier and an automatic nickname such as “Guest_1234” are created.
  • User identifier: a unique code (UID) assigned by Firebase to every user, guests included.
  • Game data: experience points (XP), level and, for each completed match, the game mode, points, correct answers, number of questions and date.
  • On-device data: your chosen language and your sign-in session are stored locally on your device.

We do not collect location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or payment data. We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking tools.

Why we process it and on what legal basis

  • To create and manage your account and let you play, saving your progress and match history: performance of the service you request (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • For the security and proper functioning of the service: legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Who we share it with

Data is stored on Google Firebase services (Authentication and Cloud Firestore), which acts as our service provider (data processor). We do not sell your data and do not share it for advertising purposes. Other users cannot see your profile or your matches.

Google may process data outside the European Economic Area (for example in the United States) on the basis of the safeguards provided by the GDPR (standard contractual clauses and/or an adequacy decision).

How long we keep it

For as long as you keep your account. When you request account deletion, we delete your profile and match history.

Your rights

You may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction and portability of your data, and object to processing, by writing to info.fc.developer@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in Italy: www.garanteprivacy.it).

How to delete your account and data

Write to info.fc.developer@gmail.com from the email address of your account (or stating your nickname), with the subject “Kit & Kick account deletion”. We delete the account and linked data within 30 days.

Minimum age

The app is not directed at children. In Italy, using an online service that requires registration requires being at least 14 years old, or having the consent of a parent or guardian.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we process data (for example by adding public leaderboards), we will update this page and the date above.
